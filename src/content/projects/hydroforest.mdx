## Overview

HydroForest is a water tracking app that makes drinking enough water a bit of a game. Every time you log a bottle, the tree you picked grows a stage, and over time you build up your own forest. The app keeps your current and longest daily streak, shows your history in a calendar, and lets you add friends and compare with them on a leaderboard.

We built it as a team of five.

## How it's built

- **Frontend:** React with React Router, and Framer Motion for the animations, like the tree growing when you water it.
- **Backend:** Node.js and Express with a MongoDB database through Mongoose.
- **Accounts:** sign up and sign in with JWT through Passport, passwords hashed with bcrypt, and every input checked with express-validator. You can change your email or password, or reset it if you forget it.
- **Tests:** 26 Mocha and Chai tests for the API, with coverage reports from c8.
- **Docker** for running the backend.
