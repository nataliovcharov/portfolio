## Overview

Voice Journal is a hands-free journal. You record an entry, the app transcribes your speech,
analyzes the sentiment of what you said, and tracks your mood over time in a chart with a
history of every entry.

## How it's built

- **Machine learning client** that handles transcription and sentiment analysis.
- **Flask web app** for recording, uploading and browsing entries.
- **MongoDB** for storing transcripts and sentiment results.
- Each subsystem runs in its own **Docker** container inside a monorepo, with its own **GitHub Actions** pipeline for testing, publishing images to Docker Hub, and deploying to DigitalOcean.

## What I practised

Integrating an ML component into a real web application, designing the data flow between
services, and containerizing a multi-service system.
