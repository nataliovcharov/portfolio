## Overview

With my team, I wrote a complete specification for a mobile app of our choice — the
groundwork a product team does before writing any code.

## What we produced

- **Ideation and requirements analysis**
- **Product vision statement**
- **User stories**
- **UML diagrams**
- **A clickable Figma prototype**
- **A final presentation** for stakeholders

## What I practised

Turning a loose idea into clear requirements, and communicating a product to people who
aren't engineers.
